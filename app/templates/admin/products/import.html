How to lay out your file

The easiest file is a spreadsheet with these column headings on the first row:

Product NameCategorySelling PriceStock
Jollof RiceMain Meals2,50020
ZoboDrinks1,00050

Only Product Name and Selling Price are needed. The other columns are optional, and you can rename the headings (Item, Amount, Price, Unit Price and so on all work).

  • For a Word or PDF file, a simple list works too - one product per line, like Jollof Rice - 2500.
  • Prices may be written as 2500, 2,500.00 or ₦2,500.
  • Existing products are matched by SKU or barcode, and then by name, so re-uploading an updated list changes prices instead of creating duplicates.
  • If a row has no Category, the product is filed automatically from its name (Jollof Rice goes to Main Meals, Zobo to Drinks, and so on), falling back to 'Other'.
  • A category in your file that the shop does not have yet is created for you.
